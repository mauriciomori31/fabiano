# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults seeking dental care who value both technological advancement and personalized, humanized service. Likely aged 25-65, concerned about oral health, aesthetics, and long-term dental wellbeing. They research clinics online before booking appointments and look for signs of expertise, modern equipment, and patient-centered care.

## Product Purpose

To convert website visitors into patient appointments by showcasing the clinic's blend of cutting-edge dental technology and genuinely compassionate patient care. The site aims to build trust, reduce anxiety about dental visits, and highlight specialized services that address specific patient needs.

## Brand Personality

Professional, modern, trustworthy, caring, technological yet humanized. The clinic presents as expert-led but warm, innovative but approachable.

## Anti-references

Outdated dental clinic websites with stock photos, generic templates, lacking clear service differentiation, poor mobile experience, or that feel cold and clinical without human warmth. Sites that focus only on procedures without addressing patient emotions or comfort.

## Design Principles

1. Balance technological sophistication with approachable warmth
2. Lead with patient benefits rather than technical features
3. Use visual hierarchy to guide users toward booking appointments
4. Maintain consistency in dental imagery and color psychology (trust blues, health greens, energy accents)
5. Prioritize mobile experience given significant health-related searches occur on phones

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA compliance as baseline. Considerations for:
- Color blindness (avoid relying solely on color to convey information)
- Keyboard navigation for motor impairments
- Screen reader compatibility (proper heading structure, alt text, aria labels)
- Reduced motion preferences (respect CSS prefers-reduced-motion)
- Clear, legible typography for older users
